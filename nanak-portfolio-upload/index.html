Floor 1. Label design project, 2019.

White Rhino Brewing Co.

Stout is the serious beer on the shelf. I made it the fun one.

Third prizeWhite Rhino Brewing Co.
February 2019

The brief

A limited-edition stout label with the brand's bold personality, built to stand out on shelf and speak to a younger craft beer crowd.

The idea

Skip barley, barrels and brewery heritage. Borrow from 1980s vapourwave instead: neon gradients, palm trees, a pink retro phone, and the line Shout for Stout. Sharp. Strong. Dark.

The craft

A moodboard locked the palette, then the full wraparound label, legal and product information included, without losing the energy.

What happened

Third prize, awarded by director Ishaan Puri in February 2019.

Floor 3. Punjab National Bank, designed at BCG.

PNB One

An instant loan should feel instant.

How it started

I first designed concept posters for the PNB One app launch. They were approved by the consulting team but not rolled out, and PNB then asked for me by name.

The brief

Promote pre-approved personal loans on the app across five formats, from Instagram posts to standees, in one week.

The idea

A hand reaching out of a phone with a bag of money: no branch, no paperwork, the loan comes to you. Two routes, a photographic real hand and an illustrated one with a stopwatch for speed.

What happened

Both ran. The real-hand route got the most visibility, on billboards across Delhi and on PNB's social channels, and I went on to work on PNB's longer-term projects.

Floor 8. Master of Design, University of Auckland, 2025. Solo project.

Climate × Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights

Climate money flows everywhere except to the women it affects most.

The brief

Design a framework showing how climate change threatens women's sexual and reproductive health, and how investors could respond. Framework proposed for Organon × Y-Labs.

The idea

Turn a policy framework into a visual argument. Scales show women carrying the heavier risk. A funnel shows climate funding trickling past them. A handshake shows what happens when investors work together.

The craft

Fifteen visuals moving from problem to evidence to strategy, ending in a theory of change that maps activities to outcomes and goals.

Sources

Data from WHO, IOM and UNFPA.

Floor 9. Master of Design thesis, University of Auckland.

Designing trust in cosmetic medical tourism

Trust starts before a patient ever walks through the door.

The question

How digital touchpoints build or break trust, empathy and cultural sensitivity in the pre-visit patient experience of cosmetic medical tourism.

The framework

SIGN, for Sensitization, Involvement, Navigation and Guidance: a diagnostic tool for evaluating digital trust and cultural sensitivity before a first visit.

The method

I audited nine cosmetic clinics across South Korea, Turkey and Colombia against a custom evaluation matrix built from research and existing toolkits, using a custom Gemini Gem fed with my own criteria to speed up the analysis.

What happened

Three industry experts validated the methodology. I'm co-writing the thesis into a journal article with my supervisor, and developed it into a venture concept through the University's Innovation Hatchery.
